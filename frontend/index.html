🔬 What is UV-C Light?
UV-C is ultraviolet light with wavelengths of 100–280 nanometers — shorter than visible light and invisible to the human eye. At appropriate doses, UV-C radiation can damage the DNA/RNA of microorganisms, potentially inactivating bacteria, viruses, and mold spores.
✅ What UV-C May Do
  • Inactivate certain bacteria and viruses under suitable conditions
  • Reduce microbial load on surfaces (direct-irradiation systems)
  • Treat air passing through enclosed UV-C chambers
  • Complement other filtration methods
❌ What UV-C Does NOT Do
  • Does not remove PM2.5 particles (need a particle filter for that)
  • Does not remove CO₂ or gases
  • Does not guarantee sterilization in all conditions
  • Effectiveness depends on distance, exposure time, and shielding
  • Does not replace HEPA filtration or ventilation
⚠️ Critical Safety Information
Direct UV-C exposure is hazardous to humans and animals.
Even brief exposure to germicidal UV-C can cause eye damage (photokeratitis) and skin burns. Only enclosed UV-C systems — where the lamp is fully shielded from direct human or pet exposure — are appropriate for home use.

Enclosed systems treat air that passes through an internal chamber. The UV-C lamp never directly irradiates the room occupants or surfaces visible to them.
🚨 SAQSHY AIR will never automatically activate any UV-C device. UV-C equipment must only be operated according to the manufacturer's safety instructions. Never expose people or pets to direct germicidal UV-C light.
